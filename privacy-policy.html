SELLER HUB

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information Seller Hub uses and why. In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and your data stays on your phone unless you send us a message.

Information on your phone

Your list of shops, the links you add, your language, your sales, products, notes and reminders, and your boost purchase history are saved only on your phone. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Website icons

When you add your own shop, the app downloads that website's icon through Google's favicon service. For this, the shop's web address is sent to Google. Nothing else about you is sent. Google's privacy policy applies to this request.

Purchases

Boosts are paid through Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details. The store tells the app which boosts your account has bought, so the app can show and restore them. The store's privacy policy applies to payments.

Shops you open

When you open a shop, it opens in that shop's app or in your browser. What happens there is covered by that shop's own privacy policy.

Contacting support

If you send us a message from Help & support, your name, email address and message are sent to us through Formspree, an email form service, together with the app version, language and phone type so we can help. We use them only to answer you.

What we don't do

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking. We don't create accounts. We don't sell or share any data.

Children

Seller Hub is made for people who sell online and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email us at sellerhub.boost@gmail.com.
